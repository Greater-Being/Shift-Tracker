# Shift Tracker & Dog Sitting Calculator (משמרות וכלבים)

A lightweight, single-file Progressive Web App (PWA) for tracking work shifts, dog-sitting days, and monthly income estimates[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). Built in Hebrew (RTL) with a dark, mobile-first interface[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

## Features

* **Interactive Monthly Calendar:** View the full month at a glance, including weekends, with quick visual indicators for worked days and dog-sitting days[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
* **One-Tap Daily Logging:** Toggle shift status (using default shift hours or custom overrides per day), mark dog-sitting days, and add optional notes[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
* **Customizable Rates & Deductions:** Configure hourly or flat shift rates, daily dog-sitting bonuses, travel allowances, and estimated tax/credit point deductions with effective-date history[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
* **Monthly Reports & Export:**
  * Instant WhatsApp-formatted summary (copy to clipboard or open directly in WhatsApp)[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
  * Full monthly breakdown exportable to CSV/Excel[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
* **Offline & Local Storage:** All data is stored locally in the browser (`localStorage`) with built-in JSON backup and restore[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
