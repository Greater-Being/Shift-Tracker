# Shift Tracker & Dog Sitting Calculator (משמרות וכלבים)

A lightweight, single-file Progressive Web App (PWA) for tracking work shifts, dog-sitting days, and monthly income estimates. Built in Hebrew (RTL) with a dark, mobile-first interface.

## Features

* **Interactive Monthly Calendar:** View the full month at a glance, including weekends, with quick visual indicators for worked days and dog-sitting days.
* **One-Tap Daily Logging:** Toggle shift status (using default shift hours or custom overrides per day), mark dog-sitting days, and add optional notes.
* **Customizable Rates & Deductions:** Configure hourly or flat shift rates, daily dog-sitting bonuses, travel allowances, and estimated tax/credit point deductions with effective-date history.
* **Monthly Reports & Export:**
  * Instant WhatsApp-formatted summary (copy to clipboard or open directly in WhatsApp).
  * Full monthly breakdown exportable to CSV/Excel.
* **Offline & Local Storage:** All data is stored locally in the browser (`localStorage`) with built-in JSON backup and restore.
